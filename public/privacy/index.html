What we store

  • Account details (if you create an account): your first name, email address and password (stored encrypted).

Health information is a special category of data. We only store it with your explicit consent, which you give when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

Why we store it

Only to give you the app: your daily targets, logs, progress and suggested exercises. We don't sell your data, show ads or use it for marketing.

Where it is kept

  • On your phone, in your browser's storage.
  • In your account, if you have one, so it follows you to another phone. Accounts and data are stored with Supabase ([region, e.g. EU – Frankfurt]).
  • The app is hosted by Netlify.

Services that see some of your data

  • AI features (meal estimates, food ideas, workout builder, coach): what you type or photograph for that feature is sent to Anthropic to produce the answer.
  • Barcode lookups: the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts. No personal information is included.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting your account (Profile & settings → Delete my account) permanently removes your account and everything saved with it.
